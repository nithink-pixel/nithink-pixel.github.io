01 / Reliable reporting foundations

RevRisk v2.0

A layered dbt warehouse connecting incremental facts, contract history, governed reporting marts, and exact-dollar revenue reconciliation.

39 declared data-quality tests

Synthetic data · Portfolio environment

dbt · DuckDB · Python · GitHub Actions

Problem, approach & limitations

The business question

Can a revenue dashboard prove that its numbers agree with source transactions and preserve changes in contract history?

My contribution

Built the transformation foundation, dimensional models, revenue controls, analytical marts, and dashboard. The repository includes incremental transaction processing, SCD Type 2 contract snapshots, CI, and an Airflow DAG.

An important engineering decision

A three-day lookback and transaction-ID merge accommodate late-arriving records without duplicating transactions. Older changes require a separate refresh strategy.

Evaluation and limitations

The source declares 38 generic tests and one singular reconciliation test. This is a count of implemented controls, not a claim that a current run has passed. The data is synthetic; modeled exposure does not represent recovered revenue.

02 / Planning & operational decisions

PlanSync

A reproducible planning pipeline connecting demand forecasts, supply feasibility, capacity actions, and an approval queue.

Forecast uncertainty to feasible actions

Simulated company · Simulated outcomes

Python · DuckDB · OR-Tools · Streamlit

Problem, approach & limitations

The business question

Which capacity and fulfillment actions should a planning team prioritize when demand exceeds available supply?

My contribution

Designed a seeded company spanning 120 SKUs, 12 regions, eight suppliers, two plants, and three distribution centers. Built forecasting, consensus, supply feasibility, optimization, scenario comparison, and decision outputs.

An important analytical decision

P10/P50/P90 forecasts communicate demand uncertainty. OR-Tools compares overtime, expediting, and transfers under modeled capacity constraints.

Evaluation and limitations

The committed output contains 373 rows. Demand-weighted fill rate is 69.779% before actions and 99.952% after. These are simulated outcomes. Some capacity limits use simplified heuristics, and root-cause attribution is rule-based rather than a formal causal decomposition.

03 / Commercial decision support

AdsPulse

A marketing analytics platform combining experiment readouts, attribution, customer cohorts, and governed KPIs.

5 attribution models compared

Synthetic campaigns · Simulated experiments

Python · DuckDB · SciPy · Streamlit

Problem, approach & limitations

The business question

How should a marketing team evaluate experiments, compare attribution assumptions, and decide which metrics to trust?

My contribution

Implemented campaign reporting, experiment analysis, attribution, cohort analytics, KPI governance, and a Streamlit interface. Synthetic inputs include known treatment effects and injected data-quality issues.

An important analytical decision

Compare attribution models separately from incrementality. Conversion credit explains an allocation rule; causal lift requires additional experimental assumptions.

Evaluation and limitations

Methods include two-proportion z-tests, confidence intervals, power analysis, and cohort analysis. Synthetic ground truth supports methodological checks. No realized advertising uplift or budget savings are claimed.

Olist E-Commerce Analytics

A Python and SQL analysis connecting order revenue, delivery performance, customer reviews, and customer segments.

Orders, delivery & customer analysis

Synthetic fallback · Olist-schema analysis

Python · SQL · Pandas · Statistical analysis

Problem, approach & limitations

The business question

How can an e-commerce team examine revenue, delivery performance, and customer behavior together?

My contribution

Built a notebook workflow combining relational tables, revenue and delivery indicators, customer reviews, and RFM segmentation.

Dataset provenance

The notebook attempts to load the Olist public dataset and includes a seeded synthetic fallback. The committed export contains 93,014 unique order IDs using the generated format from that fallback.

Evaluation and limitations

This case study covers the notebook version and treats its committed outputs as synthetic. The results demonstrate analytical methods, not observed marketplace performance or causal business effects.
